The Best Movies According to Sushma

My Top 3 movies of all-time

Zootopia

Zootopia is a very fun and entertaining movie with a great message about believing in yourself and not judging others. The characters, especially Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde, make the story even more enjoyable.

Tangled

Tangled is a fun and heartwarming movie about Rapunzel’s exciting adventure outside the tower. The animation and characters make it really enjoyable to watch.

Moana

Moana is a beautiful and inspiring movie about a brave girl who goes on a journey across the ocean to save her people. The songs and animation make the movie very enjoyable.
